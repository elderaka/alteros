# Tone: `tone.warm`

## Meaning

Caring, supportive, affectionate, or reassuring delivery.

## Use

Comfort, companionship, gratitude, encouragement, and gentle responses.

## Excludes

Warmth is not proof of romantic intent, consent, or factual correctness.

## Notes

Warm can combine with serious or urgent. Generated dialogue may declare a response tone of warm even when the player’s input tone is distressed.
