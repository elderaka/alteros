# Tone: `tone.neutral`

## Meaning

Plain, low-signal delivery without a strong emotional or rhetorical direction.

## Use

The default evidence and fallback style when no other Tone clears the catalog threshold.

## Excludes

Neutral does not mean truthful, sincere, or emotionally empty. It is only a delivery reading.

## Notes

Neutral may coexist with any Topic and Intent. It should not erase explicit user-state facts.
