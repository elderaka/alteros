# Intent: `relationship`

## Meaning

The player is expressing or inviting social closeness with Alterra.

## Includes

- Affection, hugs, compliments, gratitude, missing someone, companionship, and gentle teasing.

## Excludes

- A neutral information question (`ask`).
- Hostile, sexual, or boundary-testing content (`boundary`).

## Current route coverage

`affection_confession`, `affection_gesture`, `compliment`, `gratitude`, `missed_you`, `tease_playful`, `too_many_compliments`.

## Notes

Relationship intent does not decide the relationship outcome. User state, consent, cooldowns, and authored response rules remain authoritative.
