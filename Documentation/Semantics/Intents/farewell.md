# Intent: `farewell`

## Meaning

The player is closing the current interaction or saying goodnight.

## Includes

- Goodbye, see-you-later, and leaving statements.
- Goodnight when it functions as a conversation close.

## Excludes

- A sleep request or sleep-support topic that does not close contact (`request`).
- Checking whether Alterra is present (`presence`).

## Current route coverage

`farewell`, `goodnight`.

## Notes

`time.night` may be a local anchor. A goodnight line can also select a personalized sleep-related variant.
