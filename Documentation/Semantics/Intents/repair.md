# Intent: `repair`

## Meaning

The player is acknowledging, correcting, or closing a small conversational exchange.

## Includes

- Apologies.
- Short acknowledgements such as “okay,” “sure,” or “thanks” when no new route is requested.

## Excludes

- A substantive question or request.
- A boundary violation or hostile statement (`boundary`).

## Current route coverage

`apology`, `no_response_needed`.

## Notes

Repair is deliberately broad in the starter catalogue. It can split later if apology, correction, and acknowledgement acquire different state or response contracts.
