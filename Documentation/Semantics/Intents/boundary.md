# Intent: `boundary`

## Meaning

The player is testing, crossing, or reacting against a social or safety boundary.

## Includes

- Hostility, profanity, insults, sexual/inappropriate requests, and repeated pressure.

## Excludes

- Ordinary playful teasing that remains within the authored relationship policy (`relationship`).
- A neutral unclear line (`fallback`).

## Current route coverage

`hostility_major`, `hostility_medium`, `inappropriate_boundary`, `insult_boundary_light`, `profanity_minor`, `too_many_status_checks`.

## Notes

Boundary intent is safety-sensitive. Tone may provide supporting evidence, but it cannot weaken deterministic policy checks.
