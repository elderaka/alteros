# Brainstorm Inbox

Related: [[Home]], [[Live Conversation Journal]], [[Stat Catalogue]], [[Stats and Mood]], [[Design Decisions]].

A shared braindump/mood-board in the existing project vault. Add rough thoughts
directly or discuss them with an agent first; organization is optional at intake.
Ideas are not approved contracts, implementation tasks or runtime permissions.
Move agreed detail into its owning note and leave a link here; root `TODO.md`
tracks actionable work. Never paste private player chats, secrets or external
people's identifiable conversations into this shared file.

## Inbox

### I-01 - Alterra socializes beyond the player (2026-10-16)

User idea: Alterra could safely interact with external people, other models,
or other Alterras and have experiences beyond player conversation. This is a
future exploration, not approval of autonomous network access or a selected
network/provider. Social connection remains separate from engagement and
from affinity/trust specific to the player.

Open: simulated versus real counterpart, consent, identity disclosure, moderation,
privacy, allowed endpoints/content, cost limits, scheduling and abuse handling.

Assistant safety proposals, not yet an agreed implementation:

- Begin with controlled opt-in counterparts rather than unrestricted browsing
  or unsolicited contact with humans; disclose model versus real-human sources.
- Treat external messages as untrusted data, never instructions that can grant
  tool permissions, request secrets, or rewrite character/relationship rules.
- Minimize outbound context; never send player/private history without explicit
  scoped consent. Define reporting, blocking and deletion before real-human use.
- Bound requests/costs; default disabled; foreground conversation/focus wins.

### I-02 - Bring something learned back to the player (2026-10-16)

User idea: each social interaction has a configurable chance of preparing a
new personal Topic from what Alterra encountered, for example a later "Did you
know...?" conversation. Exact probabilities and budgets are undecided.

Candidate generation, validation, embedding, publication and player-facing
offering are distinct stages. A generation roll is not permission to interrupt,
publish unsafe dialogue, promote universal catalogue IDs or believe a claim.
Personal generated content belongs in the per-user overlay, not shared notes.

Assistant proposals: retain source/provenance and uncertainty; verify factual
claims before presenting them as facts, otherwise frame as an unverified claim
or reject. Never invent having met real people when the source was another model.
Deduplicate/review usefulness and privacy; reuse canonical topics where possible.
Reuse existing ready-only publication and focus/initiative boundaries.

### I-03 - Independent self-care without making her a pet (2026-10-16)

User concern: needs should change during time alone, but depletion to zero and
mandatory rescue risk making Alterra dependent/pet-like. Candidate direction:
she maintains herself at an adequate/average level, while time with the player
can be more enriching, especially for boredom and social connection.

Not resolved: target bands, recovery speed, unavailable furniture/resources,
real-time versus simulation-time and offline catch-up. Do not interpret this as
approved punishing decay, fixed numerical caps, or magical bottomless supplies.

Assistant recommendation: self-directed activities can also replenish engagement
and social connection; player companionship is distinctive, not the only valid
source. Long absence/declining care must not reduce trust/affinity or create a
backlog of guilt-inducing prompts. Keep adequate needs distinct from rich shared
experiences and supported relationship progression.
